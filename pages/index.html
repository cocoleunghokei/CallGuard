Callguard

A Mac app that listens to work calls, spots scam scripts as they happen, checks them against the firm's own rules, and warns you before you pay, share a code or change bank details.

  • Runs on the laptopSpeech-to-text and the AI model run locally. No call audio or words are stored or sent anywhere.
  • One thing goes onlineOnly the name of the organization the caller claims to be (e.g. "Department of Justice") is looked up to find its real public number. Every lookup shows in the app's "Sent online" log.
  • Works offlineWith the Wi-Fi off, everything still works except that lookup.
